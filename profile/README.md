<h1 align="center">Poolser</h1>

<p align="center">Keep an eye on your liquidity.</p>

Poolser helps you monitor Uniswap v3 and v4 positions: portfolio value, unclaimed fees, fee history and price ranges across saved wallets.

[Download for Mac](https://apps.apple.com/app/poolser/id6762252798) · [Product details](https://github.com/poolser-app/native#readme) · [Support](https://github.com/poolser-app/native/issues) · [Privacy](https://github.com/poolser-app/native/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)

## Repositories

| Repository | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [native](https://github.com/poolser-app/native) | Native SwiftUI apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad, plus widgets. Fee collection is available on Mac. |
| [web](https://github.com/poolser-app/web) | The browser application. In development. |
| [website](https://github.com/poolser-app/website) | The public product website, downloads, support and privacy pages. In development. |
| [brand](https://github.com/poolser-app/brand) | Shared brand guidelines, original artwork, app icons and colour tokens. |

The native README has the current platform availability and setup requirements. The native apps use your own Infura key and public wallet addresses. Monitoring is read-only; when collecting fees on Mac, your browser wallet reviews and signs the transaction. Poolser never sees your private keys or signs transactions.
